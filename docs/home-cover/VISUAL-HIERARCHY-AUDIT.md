# Home — cover, search, index

Baseline: `e6d51860e69382241fd0d8fdeda5480bf3ec9ff8`.

## Observed before editing

The actual Home was captured at 360, 768, 1440 and 3440 px. The desktop and mobile images were read before changing presentation. Screenshots are local QA artifacts under `outputs/home-cover/before-*.png`.

- At 360 px the identity was only 24 px; at 1440/3440 it was 36 px. The sidebar logo had more presence than the Home identity.
- Home used a paper background, a black search field, one yellow underline, and three similarly weighted lists. It read as an index rather than the front cover of this particular tool.
- All 23 existing navigation buttons worked as a useful inventory. They do not need new destinations or decorative icons.
- Search already has the correct immediate action. Source/result reading and inline Korean helpers are outside this visual change.

## Intended hierarchy

1. **Identity + search:** an acid-yellow masthead with oversized existing blackletter, immediately attached to the black search strip. No landing-page introduction.
2. **Reference + play:** a black reference section; a restrained magenta marker for Play; large, contrasting typographic section headings. Existing buttons remain direct.
3. **Codex + management:** readable paper-based lists, with campaign and optional records below. No equal-card dashboard or added pictograms.

Art direction: a printed cover and working index. Color comes from meaningful surfaces; typography carries identity. No random rotation, new fonts, grunge assets, new content, or altered generator behavior. The mobile composition must retain readable Korean and at least 44 px action targets. Source/inspector pages keep their current restrained styling.
