# MÖRK BORG — Reference Desk and Campaign Codex

The final product direction follows the later instruction: a compact, source-linked rules and Oracle workbench beside a handwritten campaign notebook. Search, contextual tools, related tables, clear results, and copy access are the primary workflow. Sessions and the campaign chronicle remain available under the collapsed **보조 기록** navigation; using the reference desk does not require them.

The original campaign-memory work was retained rather than discarded. City support adds reference procedures and temporary readings. It does not introduce a Settlement entity, a city journal, or automatic narrative expansion.

## Source and architecture notes

The canonical Oracle registry remains the table-data authority. The reference index stores IDs, keywords, contexts, source chains and actionable links. It does not build a second copy of every rulebook table. Long Businesses and NPC entries from Alöne were extracted from their own bounded PDF cells to prevent adjacent columns from merging. All source-bearing Alöne pages were visually verified; repeated source outcomes and conditional branches were retained.

The nine indexed books are MÖRK BORG Bare Bones, MÖRK BORG Full Edition, FERETORY, HERETIC, Sölitary Defilement, Sölitary Depths, RECLVSE, Mythic Game Master Emulator Second Edition, and Alöne in the Crowd. Source tables remain in the existing private data route, while public code carries numeric procedures, short original explanations, IDs and source metadata. No borrowed illustrations or extracted logos were added; the existing local fonts retain their bundled SIL Open Font License files.

Reference preferences use a separate `morkborg-reference-desk:v1` key, bounded to 30 pins and 10 recent references. Reading/copy operations remain transient. The v6 campaign schema supports the optional record workflow without making it a prerequisite for source lookup.

## Play-friction result

The final 18-step paper-notebook scenario required **18 pointer clicks plus one full-page reload/navigation**. Two additional verification clicks opened the unchanged campaign export. The initial Reaction and Action/Theme pins had already passed pin/reload testing; this run added Failed Morale from Recent. Source disclosure and closing inspectors are included in the 18 clicks. Regional monster generation takes 2 clicks from the desk, ordinary/pinned/related rollers 1, parameterized room encounters 2, and copy/back/reroll 1 each.

The final question is answered with a practical qualification: this desk removes repeated PDF searching for the indexed, verified material and works beside a paper notebook. It still exposes missing or contradictory source instructions instead of pretending to cover them. Full rulebook nuance, rulings, unprovided appendices and narrative decisions remain outside automated results.
